Final Step Verify connectivity from VPCS console
Open the console of any VPCS node in GNS3 and run these commands to confirm everything is working:
Check your assigned IP, mask and gateway: show ip
Ping your router gateway to confirm the PC is connected: ping <gateway IP>
Ping a PC in a different subnet to confirm routing works between subnets: ping <other PC's IP>
All three should succeed. If ping fails across subnets, check that Deploy ran on all routers and re-run Audit.
